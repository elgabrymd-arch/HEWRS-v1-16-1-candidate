## Packaging and installation

The cumulative COPY_TO_APP folder includes the previous updates, all active item thumbnails, the B15 source photo and the new blazer layer/mask. Merge its CONTENTS into the existing app root containing index.html, retaining unrelated files. Do not copy only index.html and do not nest COPY_TO_APP inside the app.

The included apply_update.py performs a read-only dry run unless --apply is specified. A new backup directory outside the app is required for apply; rollback refuses later edits and does not touch browser data. The package includes the executed installation/rollback result separately. Nothing is published merely by downloading the ZIP.
